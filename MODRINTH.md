# XPTracker

**Track every point of XP you earn and find out where it comes from.**

XPTracker is a lightweight, client-side Fabric mod that monitors your experience gains in real time, breaks them down by source, and shows everything in a clean, configurable HUD overlay. Whether you're grinding a mob farm, strip mining, or trading with villagers — XPTracker keeps count so you can measure and optimize your farming.

---

## Features

### Session Tracking
- **Total XP gained** this session
- **Level gains** since you started
- **Session time** with auto-pause
- **XP per minute** efficiency in real time

### Automatic Source Detection
- **Combat** — XP from killing mobs
- **Mining** — XP from breaking ores (Coal, Diamond, Emerald, Lapis, Redstone, Nether Quartz, Nether Gold, Sculk, Spawners)
- **Smelting** — XP from furnace outputs
- **Trading** — XP from villager trades
- **Fishing** — XP from reeling in catches
- **Breeding** — XP from breeding animals
- **Percentage breakdown** — see which source contributes most

### Smart Session Management
- **Toggle tracking** on and off with a single keybind
- **Auto-pause** when the pause menu opens, auto-resume on close
- **Session persistence** across world changes (optional)
- **Chat summary** with a detailed breakdown on reset

### Customizable HUD
- Four corner positions, adjustable scale
- Custom text and background colors, adjustable opacity
- Toggle individual stats to show only what you care about

---

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **J** | Toggle tracking on / off |
| **K** | Reset session |

---

## Customization

Configure the mod in-game via [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) + [YACL](https://modrinth.com/mod/yacl), or by editing `.minecraft/config/xptracker.json`. Settings are grouped into **Session**, **HUD**, and **Display** tabs.

---

## Requirements

- **Minecraft:** 26.1+
- **Fabric Loader:** 0.18.4 or higher
- **Fabric API:** Required
- **Java:** 25 or higher
- **Mod Menu + YACL:** Optional (for the in-game config screen)

---

## Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/)
2. Download [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
3. Download XPTracker
4. Place both JAR files in your `mods` folder
5. Launch Minecraft
